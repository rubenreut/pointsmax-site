Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Pointsmax collects no personal data unless you choose to join a Study Squad or a school leaderboard.

  • Your subjects, grades, progress, flashcard history and settings are stored only on your device (and in your personal iCloud backup if you have that enabled). They never reach us.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK and no advertising.
  • Study Squads and school leaderboards (optional). Only if you join a squad or turn on the school leaderboard, Pointsmax saves a small profile in Apple's CloudKit (iCloud) so your squad can see it: a random player ID, a made-up display name and colour (you can't type your own name or messages), your weekly study effort, levels done and which days you studied, and, only if you opt in, your school and county. Other squad members and leaderboard viewers can see this. It is used only to run squads and leaderboards, is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to track you. You need to be signed in to iCloud to use squads; we never see your Apple ID, name or email. To have your squad data deleted, email us at the address below with your squad's join code.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information from a purchase.
  • Tapping "Open in paper" opens the State Examinations Commission's public website in your browser; their privacy policy applies there.
